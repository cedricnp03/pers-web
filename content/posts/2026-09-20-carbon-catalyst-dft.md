During my master's degree in computational chemistry, my primary research centered on Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations to investigate the catalytic polymerization of undecenol.

The main challenge in catalyst design is balancing surface reactivity with selectivity. By tailoring the electronic structure and functional groups on carbon frameworks, we explored how activation barriers change across the reaction pathway.

Beyond the chemistry itself, this work taught me how to write automated Python data pipelines, manage remote compute jobs across supercomputer clusters, and approach debugging with a strictly empirical mindset.
