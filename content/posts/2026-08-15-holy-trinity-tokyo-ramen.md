---

Living in Tokyo means having access to incredible food, but for me, nothing competes with heavy, fatty ramen:

1. **Jirokei (二郎系)**: Thick, unruly, chewy noodles buried beneath a mountain of bean sprouts, cabbage, chopped raw garlic, and braised pork chunks. It is an endurance sport, not just a meal.
2. **Iekei (家系)**: The Yokohama gift to the world. Rich tonkotsu-shoyu broth, thick straight noodles, spinach, and a layer of golden chicken oil (*chiyu*). Always ordered with a side of white rice to soak up the soup with broth-dipped nori.
3. **Abura Soba (油そば)**: A soupless bowl of oil, tare, and chewy noodles. The most efficient type of food in the world, low in volume, high in calories.  

I'm not limiting myself to these three, although these make up the majority of my dining out expenses.

I will be logging my favorite spots across Tokyo here.
