In a few weeks, I officially start my first full-time role as a software engineer in Tokyo.

Transitioning from computational chemistry to software engineering feels both natural and strange. In the lab, you spend days setting up simulations on HPC clusters, monitoring SCF convergence, and waiting for molecular structures to optimize. 

In software, the feedback loop is drastically faster. You write a function, run tests, and immediately know whether your hypothesis holds.

This website is where I plan to dump my raw thoughts, research reflections, music notes, and ramen logs as I navigate this new chapter.
